# Proof checks for the lecture models

19 November 2026. This report supersedes the blanket source qualifications for the compact-operator example in the earlier audit. The liquid identification remains open in this review. These are mathematical argument checks, not formal verification or independent human review.

## Results established

1. The compact-operator kernel has the stated intrinsic Hilbert-space model, provided one takes the **stable** subcategory generated under colimits. The previous wording omitted stable closure. Degree-zero Hilbert telescopes and their colimits alone stay connective and cannot generate the entire stable kernel.
2. The same algebraic construction works for the ideal `S_0+`, the intersection of all Schatten classes. In particular, it is flat as a right module over `B(H)`, is idempotent, and its quotient map is a homological epimorphism. It is not a closed C*-ideal, so the closed-ideal theorem was not an adequate justification; the misleading cross-reference in the liquid example was removed.
3. Both kernels carry the completed Hilbert tensor product as a commutative algebra in `Pr^dual`. Checking closure under tensor products alone would not establish this: the proof also checks the compact unit and gives a colimit-preserving right adjoint to multiplication.

The full proof is in `Chapters/05_Examples.tex`, `prop:Operator_Ideal_Models`. It is a new argument added when preparing these notes, with AI assistance, for descriptions attributed to Clausen's lectures.

## Argument and checks

Fix an infinite-dimensional separable complex Hilbert space H and set A = B(H). Work temporarily with right modules. The additive category of separable Hilbert spaces is equivalent to the category of finitely generated projective right A-modules: use Hom(H,-), bounded idempotents and their closed images. Thus its split bounded derived category has Ind-completion D(Mod-A). A complex-linear transpose identifies this with the left-module convention and preserves both ideals. This auxiliary choice is not part of the intrinsic Hilbert category.

For either ideal J, every countable family a_n in J has a positive injective common left factor d in J. Form a sufficiently rapidly weighted sum s of a_n a_n*, including a fixed injective positive element. Choose weights ensuring convergence in operator norm, and, for S_0+, in each Schatten quasi-norm with exponent 1/m. Then d = sqrt(s) works: the operator inequality a_n a_n* <= lambda_n^{-1}s gives the bounded factorization a_n = d b_n. The proof includes the elementary bounded-extension argument rather than assuming a division by d exists.

It follows that J is a directed union of free principal right ideals dA. This proves flatness. Positive square roots and polar decomposition prove J squared equals J. The Tor calculation establishes the homological epimorphism.

For positive injective d in J, the union of d^(1/2^n)A is a telescope of free modules whose transition maps lie in J. These telescope submodules form a directed covering family of J, by the countable common-factor construction. On the other hand, the kernel is stably generated by J, since its coreflector is tensoring with J. This proves the kernel description. Conversely, every J-transition telescope is killed by extension of scalars to A/J.

The tensor product on the ambient Ind-category preserves compact objects, so its right adjoint R preserves colimits. If i is the kernel inclusion and r its colimit-preserving right adjoint, the right adjoint to the restricted multiplication is (r tensor r) R i. This proves strong continuity. The unit is the one-dimensional Hilbert space; its identity lies in J, and it is compact in the kernel. Tensor closure follows from compact approximation or the product formula for Schatten singular values.

These arguments were reread for the following possible failures: the direction of the module action; injectivity of the positive factors; closure of S_0+ under roots; convergence in all Schatten classes; directedness of the telescope family; the need for stable generation; and the distinction between tensor closure and strong continuity.

## What the KU assertion means

The lecture and the current example construct an E_infinity-ring structure on the continuous K-theory spectrum, whose underlying spectrum is identified with KU. The model proof supplies the categorical input to this construction, using the multiplicativity of continuous K-theory already invoked in the lecture.

My earlier conversational description added a stronger requirement: identifying this with the conventional E_infinity multiplication on KU. The cited lecture passage does not explicitly assert that stronger identification. This check does not prove it, and the manuscript does not claim it. An equivalence of underlying spectra alone would not prove it.

## The remaining liquid identification

The unresolved assertion is the equivalence between liquid rigidification and the all-Schatten kernel. We now have a proof of the operator-algebra side, not a proof identifying it with the analytic category.

There is a concrete candidate functor: send a Hilbert space to its associated liquid complex vector space, extend from the split bounded derived category, and then extend by colimits to its Ind-completion. Restrict this realization functor to the all-Schatten kernel. To establish the displayed equivalence through this functor one still needs:

1. Derived full faithfulness on that kernel, including higher mapping groups and the behavior of mapping spectra on its generating telescopes.
2. Identification of its essential image with liquid rigidification. This requires both the analytic generation statement and the passage from nuclear objects to the rigidification under the relevant hypotheses.
3. If a symmetric monoidal equivalence is intended, compatibility of realization with the completed Hilbert tensor product on this subcategory and the liquid tensor product. This is an additional assertion, not a consequence of agreement on vector spaces or of the operator-ideal proof.

The written analytic source gives substantial related results: [Clausen–Scholze, *Condensed mathematics and complex geometry*, Lecture VIII](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/scholze/Complex.pdf), particularly Lemma 8.12, Theorem 8.15 and Corollary 8.18, describes nuclear objects using dual nuclear Fréchet spaces and proves independence of the liquid parameter. Those results do not, as stated there, identify the derived operator kernel or its mapping spectra.

One tempting shortcut fails. A sequence of Hilbert–Schmidt maps cannot in general be made to have all-Schatten transitions merely by taking a cofinal subsequence. For example, let every transition on l2 be diag(1/n). Every finite composite is diag(1/n^k), which fails to be p-Schatten when kp <= 1. Thus none of these composites belongs to S_0+. This is not a counterexample to Clausen's categorical equivalence, but it rules out a direct deduction by that shortcut.

The liquid formula consequently keeps the user's requested direct attribution to Clausen. The unresolved proof obligation is recorded here and in the ledger, rather than adding an obtrusive disclaimer to the exposition. No authorial choice is needed to finish this check: the remaining issue is mathematical, not a preference about wording.
