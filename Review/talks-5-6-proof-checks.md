## Findings and repairs

1. **Filtered-colimit construction.** The argument in `lem:Filtered_Colimits_PrL`(2) works. Added the transition maps induced by adjunction units and explained how common cofinal tails assemble the components into a functor. Made the variance explicit: the diagonal into P is cofinal, so its opposite is initial for the mapping-spectrum limit. Identified the resulting adjunction unit and the canonical cocone into F_j^R F_i.
2. **Fully faithful filtered colimits.** Replaced “One checks” by the actual map: after applying F_j^R and F_i, the unit is the filtered colimit of the whiskered units of the fully faithful stage functors. These are isomorphisms. Added why the transition inclusions in the filtered-union corollary are strongly continuous.
3. **Calkin functoriality.** Replaced the disclaimer about higher coherences with a construction using the natural colimit transformation, its invertible mates, functorial cofibers in Pr^LL, and compact objects. Checked the kernel description and its compatibility with the quotient functor. The proof of short-exactness uses both the cofiber diagram and full faithfulness of the restricted Ind functor, not an unsupported exactness assertion about Pr^LL.
4. **Compact objects and categorical foundations.** Added why limits and filtered colimits of Cat_perf are computed in Cat, why filtered colimits commute with finite limits there, and why the core preserves filtered colimits. The brief verification of the Cat assertions explicitly uses the quasicategory model. Added a model-independent proof that the core detects equivalences of stable categories: each mapping anima is a retract of the automorphism anima of a biproduct, via shear matrices. The claim would be false for arbitrary categories without the additive structure.
5. **Automatic dualizability.** Checked the joint-conservativity argument, transfer of (AB6), the alternative compact-exhaustion proof, products, and the image factorization. No change of hypotheses was necessary.
6. **Semiorthogonal decompositions.** Checked the extension triangle, the adjunctions of the projections, and the argument that their right adjoints preserve colimits. Both Fun^LL decompositions have the stated order. Their proof uses only stability and the adjunction/extension identities, not presentability of Fun^LL. Made this size-independent setting explicit in `rem:SOD_Small`.
7. **Kernels and pullbacks.** Checked the rational left-continuous refinement, the maximal dualizable subcategory, kernel universal property, and both parts of `prop:Dualizable_Pullbacks`. With these notes' gluing convention, Phi i_A is Sigma F. When G is a localization, Phi^R(c)=(0,G^R c,0), and the right adjoint of projection to A is a -> (a,G^R F(a),counit inverse). These formulas and the strong-continuity arguments are consistent. No convention change was needed.
8. **Expository duplication.** Kept `prop:Q_Exhaustion`: it supplies the left-continuity condition needed in the next lemma. Its proof refers back to compact interpolation, so the interpolation proof is not duplicated.
