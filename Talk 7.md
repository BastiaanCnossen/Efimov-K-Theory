# General limits and infinite products of dualizable categories

Throughout, we let $\kappa$ be an uncountable regular cardinal.

## Limits in $\Pr^{\dual}$

**Lemma:** If $C$ is a dualizable category and $x \in C^{\omega}$, then $y(x) = \hat{y}(x)$.
**Proof:** The adjunction unit $\id \iso \colim \simeq y$ gives a canonical comparison map $\hat{y}(x) \to y(x)$. Now, for any other $Y = \colim_{i \in I} y_i \in \Ind(C^{\kappa})$, we have$$\Hom_{\Ind(C^{\kappa})}(y(x),Y) \to \Hom_{\Ind(C^{\kappa})}(\hat{y}(x),Y),$$which becomes equivalent to$$\colim_{i \in I} \Hom_C(x,y_i) \to \Hom_C(x,\colim_i y_i),$$which is an isomorphism because $x$ is compact.

**Lemma:** There is an adjunction$$\mathrm{inc}\colon \Pr^{\dual} \rightleftarrow \PrL_{\st,\kappa} \noloc \Ind((-)^{\kappa})$$**Proof:** Given a dualizable category $C$, we have a functor $\hat{y}\colon C \to \Ind(C^{\kappa})$. This is functorial in $C$. Moreover, for $D \in \PrL_{\st,\kappa}$, there is a colimit functor $\colim\colon \Ind(D^{\kappa}) \to D$, which is a functor in $\PrL_{\st,\kappa}$. The composite$$C \xrightarrow{\hat{y}} \Ind(C^{\kappa}) \xrightarrow{\colim} C$$ is the identity because the colimit functor is a localization. Moreover, the composite$$\Ind(D^{\kappa}) \xrightarrow{\hat{y} = \Ind(\y)} \Ind(\Ind(D^{\kappa})^{\kappa}) \xrightarrow{\Ind(\colim^{\kappa})} \Ind(D^{\kappa})$$is $\Ind(-)$ applied to $D \to \Ind(D^{\kappa}) \to D$, and this composite is the identity. This shows both triangle identities are satisfied.

**Theorem:** $\Pr^{\dual}$ admits all limits.
**Proof:** Consider a diagram $C\colon I \to \Pr^{\dual}$ and let $D \in \Pr^{\dual}$. We consider the following pullback diagram:$$\begin{CD} \lim_{i \in I}\Hom_{\Pr^{\dual}}(D,C_i) @>>> \lim_{i \in I} \Hom_{\Pr^{\dual}}(D,\Ind(C_i^{\kappa})) \\ 
@VVV & @VVV \\
* @>0>> \lim_{i \in I} \Hom_{\Pr^{\dual}}(D,\Ind(\Calk_{\kappa}^{\cont}(C))) \\
@VVV & @VVV \\
* @>0>> \prod_{i \in I} \Hom_{\Pr^{\dual}}(D,\Ind(\Calk_{\kappa}^{\cont}(C))), \end{CD}$$where the second square is a pullback square as the component of $0$ is contractible. Note that the top-right entry is equivalent to $\Hom_{\Pr^{\dual}}(D,\Ind(\lim_{i \in I} C_i^{\kappa}))$, where we use that $\Ind((-)^{\kappa})$ preserves limits as it is a right adjoint. Now, recall the following facts:
(a) If $C$ is dualizable and $K \subseteq A$ is a localizing subcategory, then there exists a maximal dualizable subcategory $D \subseteq K$ such that the inclusion $D \hookrightarrow A$ is strongly continuous.
(b) If $T$ is dualizable and $C \in \PrL_{\st}$, if $F\colon T \to C$ is strongly continuous, then the subcategory $\im(F) \subseteq C$ (the localizing subcategory generated by the essential image of $F$) is dualizable and the inclusion $\im(F) \hookrightarrow C$ is strongly continuous.

From this, we conclude that we may set$$\lim^{\dual}_i C_i := \ker^{\dual}( \Ind(\lim_{i \in I} C_i^{\kappa}) \to \prod_{i \in I} \Ind(\Calk_{\kappa}^{\cont}(C_i))).$$ (Using the dualizable kernel from last time.) This has the universal property, because by construction it also satisfies the fiber sequence$$\begin{CD} \Hom_{\Pr^{\dual}}(D,\lim^{\dual}_iC_i) @>>> \lim_{i \in I} \Hom_{\Pr^{\dual}}(D,\Ind(C_i^{\kappa})) \\ 
@VVV & @VVV \\
* @>0>> \prod_{i \in I} \Hom_{\Pr^{\dual}}(D,\Ind(\Calk_{\kappa}^{\cont}(C))), \end{CD}$$

## (AB6) in $\Pr^{\dual}$

**Proposition:** Products of exact sequences in $\Pr^{\dual}$ are exact.
**Proof:** We need to show that $\prod^{\dual}_i$ preserves localizations. So let $(F_i\colon C_i \to D_i)$ be localizations in $\Pr^{\dual}$. We need to show that $(\prod_i^{\dual}F_i)^{\vee}$ is fully faithful.

Consider $(\prod_i^{\dual} D_i)^{\vee} \xrightarrow{\inc^{\vee,L}} \Ind(\prod_i D_i^{\kappa})^{\vee} = \Ind(\prod_i D_i^{\kappa,op}).$ Under the latter identification, the functor$\Ind(\prod_i F_i^R) \colon \Ind(\prod_i D_i^{\kappa}) \to \Ind(\prod_i C_i^{\kappa})$ identifies with $$(\prod_i F_i^{\kappa})^*\colon \Ind(\prod_i D_i^{\kappa,op}) \to \Ind(\prod_i C_i^{\kappa,op}).$$Now consider the following commutative diagram:$$\begin{tikzcd} & \prod_i D_i^{\vee} \dlar \ar{rr}{\prod_i \hat{y}^{\vee,L} = \prod(- \circ \colim)} && \prod_i \Ind(D_i^{\kappa,op}) \ar{dd}{\prod(F_i^{\kappa})^* \\
(\prod_i^{\dual}D_i)^{\vee} \rar{inc^{vee,L}} \ar{dd} & \Ind(\prod_iD_i^{\kappa})^{\vee} \rar{\simeq} \ar{dd} & \Ind(\prod_i D_i^{\kappa,op}) \ar{dd} \\ 
& \prod_i C_i^{\vee} \rar & \Ind(\prod_i F_i^R) \rar & \Ind(C_i^{\kappa,op}) \\
& (\prod_i^{\dual} C_i)^{\vee} \rar & \Ind(\prod_i C_i^{\kappa})^{\vee} \rar{\simeq} & \Ind(\prod_i C_i^{\kappa,op}).\end{tikzcd}$$
